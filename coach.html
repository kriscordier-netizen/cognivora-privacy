Privacy Policy: Cognivora Coach

Version 1.0, effective 14 November 2026. Applies to the Cognivora Coach mobile app (Android, test version).

1. Who we are

The controller of your personal data is JONISA BV, trading as Project Cognivora, Jachthofweg 19, 1701 Dilbeek, Belgium. Contact for all privacy questions: info@project-cognivora.com. Website: www.project-cognivora.com.

2. What the app does

Cognivora Coach is an AI career coach. It helps you build one structured career profile (experience, education, certifications, languages, skills, a bank of career stories and your job search preferences), which you review and confirm item by item. You are always talking to an AI, not a person. The app gives suggestions; you decide. Nothing is sent or published in your name without your explicit approval.

4. Legal basis

We process your data to provide the service you request (performance of a contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for importing LinkedIn data and processing documents with an AI model, on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give in the app and, for LinkedIn, in LinkedIn's authorisation screen. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data in the app and, for LinkedIn, by removing the app's access in your LinkedIn settings.

5. Where your data is stored

In the current test version your profile, transcript and settings are stored only on your phone. Your AI service key is kept in Android's encrypted storage. When the LinkedIn connection is enabled, a small server operated by us in the European Union exchanges the LinkedIn authorisation and passes your LinkedIn data to your phone; it does not keep a copy of that data.

6. Service providers (processors)

  • AI model: to read documents and run the interview, the text and documents you provide are sent to the AI model selected in the app. By default this is Google Gemini (Google Cloud, paid service, under Google's data processing terms; Google does not use this content to improve its products). Alternatively Anthropic Claude, if you choose it.
  • Speech recognition and text-to-speech: provided by the speech services of your phone (on most Android phones: Google).
  • LinkedIn: source of your profile data, only with your consent.

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, transfers rely on the safeguards offered by those providers, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

7. How long we keep data

Data stays on your phone until you delete it in the app (Settings, "Delete all my data") or uninstall the app. AI providers process requests to answer them and keep them only as long as their terms require (for example for abuse monitoring). We do not keep your LinkedIn data on our servers.

8. No selling, no automated decisions

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising. The app does not take decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects: it makes suggestions that you review and confirm. The app does not assess emotions or personality.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. In the app you can export all your data (Settings, "Copy all my data") and delete everything (Settings, "Delete all my data"). For any other request, contact info@project-cognivora.com; we answer within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), www.dataprotectionauthority.be.

10. Security

Data is stored locally on your device, the AI key is encrypted, and all connections to service providers use encrypted HTTPS.

11. Children

The app is intended for professionals aged 18 and over.

12. Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes, for example when new features are added. The version and effective date at the top show the current version.
